FAQ

Questions, answered

What is KeyPrice?

KeyPrice helps you buy a brand-new car at a better price than the showroom. Buyers join a batch for a model. We negotiate a group deal with an authorised dealer, and you confirm the locked price at the showroom.

How much does KeyPrice cost me?

Nothing. KeyPrice is free for buyers. You keep the full discount and pay the dealer directly. No booking money comes to us.

Which cars can I buy?

Brand-new cars only, of brands sold in India. The price you pay is agreed with the dealer for your car and variant — it is not listed on this page, because every model has different variants.

Which cities?

Mumbai and Ahmedabad first. Other cities across India will follow.
